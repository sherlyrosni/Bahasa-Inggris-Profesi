Step 3

Interview Question Bank

Generate questions connected to the current practice vacancy. Questions vary across motivation, evidence, behaviour, and role situations.

Tell me about yourself.
Category: Professional introduction
Answer rule

Do not stop at the claim.

For strengths, motivation, and relevant skills:

ANSWER → EVIDENCE → RELEVANCE

For experience and challenge questions:

SITUATION → ACTION → RESULT

Your evidence can come from class projects, committees, organizations, volunteering, independent projects, or part-time work.

Self-review after every answer
1. Did I answer directly?

The first sentence should address the question.

2. Did I give evidence?

Use a specific project, activity, responsibility, or result.

3. Did I connect to the role?

Show why the example matters for this vacancy.

Remember

You do not need years of job experience to answer professionally.

You need relevant, truthful evidence. A class project, committee task, volunteer activity, or independent project can become a strong answer when you explain what you did, how you did it, and why it matters for the role.
